— Introduction
A layered approach to real firmness. Every face holds its shape at a different depth — SMAS, dermis, or the fine structure at the surface. We choose the device that reaches where your face needs it.
— Energy-based Lifting

Three signature programs.

Each program is selected in consultation with the director — matched to your individual anatomy, concern, and desired outcome. Ulthera, Thermage, and Tuneface use only authentic devices cleared by the relevant regulatory bodies.

— 01
— Focused Ultrasound

Ulthera.

Focused ultrasound carried to the SMAS layer — the foundational non-surgical lift for contour and firmness when depth is what your face needs.

HIFU SMAS FDA Cleared
— 02
— Monopolar RF

Thermage.

Monopolar radiofrequency tightening through the dermis — a single-session protocol that continues to refine elasticity over the months ahead.

RF Dermal FDA Cleared
— 03
— Monopolar RF+

Tuneface.

Advanced monopolar RF with multiple handpieces — zone-specific delivery for lines, jawline, and areas that need a finer, more tailored pass.

RF 6 Handpieces Zone-specific
