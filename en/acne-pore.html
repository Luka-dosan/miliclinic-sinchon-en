Principle

Mili's exclusive Derma Renewal approach.

Derma Renewal is a carefully planned treatment designed based on a detailed analysis of each individual's skin condition and type. Using ultra-precise microbeams, we target damaged skin while minimizing harm to the surrounding healthy tissue.

Even with the most advanced laser technology, the success of treatment depends on the speed and quality of skin regeneration. At Mili Clinic, we place strong emphasis on pre- and post-treatment soothing care to support recovery and enhance overall results.

When it comes to treating enlarged pores and acne scars, the key lies in regenerating and rebuilding new tissue in the affected areas. That's why it's crucial to plan treatments according to the appropriate healing timeline and treatment intervals — ensuring optimal skin recovery and lasting improvement.

Targeting the root cause, not just the surface.

Once pores become enlarged, they are difficult to shrink naturally. At Mili Clinic Sinchon, we identify the cause of pore enlargement, analyze skin thickness and sagging, and treat the deep layers of the skin — not just the surface. Customized treatments using Capri, Secret RF, Potenza, PDT, and Plasonic target the root, ensuring lasting results.

Understanding the Concern

Two types of pores, six types of acne.

01

Sebum Hypersecretion Type

Common in adolescence and young adulthood. Hormonal changes, stress, and temperature fluctuations trigger overactive sebum production, resulting in enlarged pores. Most common in oily and acne-prone skin — requires sebaceous gland regulation.

02

Aging Pore Type

Occurs after a certain age as skin loses density and elasticity. Gravity stretches the edges of pores downward, resulting in sagging pores. Requires elasticity restoration and dermal rebuilding, not just surface treatment.

03

Acne Classification

Comedonal, inflammatory, cystic, nodular, conglobata, and keloidalis — each requires a different treatment approach. We analyze type, skin condition, and age-related causes to provide customized treatment that minimizes recurrence.

Our Treatments

Five clinical-grade tools, combined intelligently.

Customized acne treatments are performed using a variety of advanced equipment, considering the type of acne and skin condition. Acne extrusion is performed by trained skincare specialists, followed by Triamcinolone injection by licensed medical staff — the most fundamental step in acne treatment.

Capri Laser uses a 1450nm wavelength to semi-permanently destroy sebaceous glands without damaging other tissues, reducing sebum and eliminating acne bacteria at the root. PDT (Photo Dynamic Therapy) uses a photosensitizer absorbed into the sebaceous glands and pores, then a laser to destroy bacteria and suppress sebum. Secret Laser delivers high-frequency RF energy through fine microneedles, tightening collagen and elastin while normalizing sebaceous gland activity. Plasonic reduces inflammation, sterilizes, and activates cellular immune response to normalize damaged, inflamed skin.

The right combination, at the right interval, in the right order — that's the difference between symptom suppression and structural improvement.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the most effective treatment for enlarged pores?

It depends on the type of pore — sebum hypersecretion type versus aging type require different approaches. We analyze your skin thickness, elasticity, and the cause of enlargement before recommending a combination of treatments. There is no single most effective treatment; the right combination is what works.

How long does it take to see results?

Initial improvements are visible within a few weeks, but lasting structural improvement to pores and scars typically requires multiple sessions over 2–3 months. Healing timeline drives the scheduling.

Can I treat severe acne (cystic or conglobata)?

Yes. Severe acne types like cystic and conglobata require specialized treatment including extraction, anti-inflammatory injection, and laser intervention. We address both the active inflammation and the resulting scarring.

Is the procedure painful?

Pain levels vary by treatment type. Topical anesthesia is applied before laser or RF procedures. Acne extraction and Triamcinolone injection involve minimal discomfort.

Will my acne come back after treatment?

Recurrence rates are minimized through proper diagnosis of the acne type, cause analysis, and customized treatment. However, lifestyle factors and skin type affect long-term outcomes — maintenance care is recommended.
