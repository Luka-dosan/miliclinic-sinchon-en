— Frequently Asked

Lifting Q&A.

Which lifting treatment is right for me?

The answer depends on your skin's structural condition and primary concern. We diagnose skin thickness, elasticity, and aging pattern through 3D analysis, then recommend the combination — not just one platform — that addresses the layers you actually need.

Can I combine multiple lifting treatments?

Yes — combination is often the most effective approach. Different platforms reach different layers (Ulthera reaches SMAS, OligioX heats the dermis, Eve Titan addresses muscle). Combined sessions deliver layered results that single treatments cannot match.

How long do results last?

Duration varies by platform and individual skin condition. HIFU treatments (Ulthera, Shurink) typically last 12–18 months. RF treatments last 6–12 months. With proper maintenance every 6–12 months, results compound over time.

Is there downtime?

None of our lifting platforms require recovery downtime. Washing and makeup are possible immediately after most procedures. Mild redness or tenderness may occur for a few hours but resolves naturally.

How painful are these treatments?

Discomfort levels vary. HIFU treatments involve some sensation during energy delivery. RF and laser treatments are generally comfortable. Eve Titan integrates microcurrent for very comfortable sessions. Topical anesthesia and vibration devices reduce discomfort where needed.
