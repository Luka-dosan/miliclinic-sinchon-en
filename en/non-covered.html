Administrative Fees

Non-covered items

In compliance with Korean medical regulations, we publish the fees for medical certificates and administrative documents issued at our clinic.

— Notice

The fees listed below cover administrative documents only — medical certificates, treatment records, and copies. They do not include consultation fees, examination fees, or other treatment-related costs. Cosmetic and dermatological procedure fees are quoted at the time of consultation.

— 01
Schedule of Fees

Standard fees for medical certificates and records.

Pursuant to the Standards for Medical Institution Certificate Fees (Article 4(2), Annex), the following fees represent the maximum amounts that may be charged for the issuance of administrative documents.
