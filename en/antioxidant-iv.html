The Programme

Empty the body of toxins, awaken the condition.

An intravenous nutritional programme designed and customised by Mili's medical team — calibrated to your body's specific state on the day.

Mili's antioxidant IV is an intravenous infusion that supplies antioxidant and detoxification compounds — restoring the balance of cellular nutrition.

A 1:1 consultation identifies the appropriate nutrient combination for your current state — yielding safe and effective results, while supporting healthy skin and reinforcing immunity for the prevention of infection and inflammation.
