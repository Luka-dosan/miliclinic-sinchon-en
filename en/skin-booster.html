System

Mili's elasticity & texture treatment system.

Drawing on extensive clinical experience, every skin booster session at Mili Clinic is preceded by a careful diagnosis and consultation — designed to deliver the optimal effect from a single session.

A skin booster is an anti-ageing treatment that helps slow ageing and supports skin regeneration — delivering ingredients that improve the skin's environment, accelerating recovery and visible improvement of skin condition.

When choosing a skin booster, accurately understanding your current skin condition and selecting the most appropriate solution is essential. At Mili Clinic, we follow a principle of authentic, exact, and precise treatment — and use a wide range of skincare devices to deliver complex, optimised solutions for sustained post-treatment results.

Penetration

Nutrients delivered directly to where they work.

The booster is delivered directly into the skin — bypassing the surface barrier — so that the active ingredients reach the layers that drive healing and regeneration from within.

For optimal effect, the injection device itself is selected to suit the booster type and treatment goal — single-needle, multi-needle injector, or dermal MTS — chosen with precision rather than convention.
