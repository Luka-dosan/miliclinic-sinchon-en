Regeneration

Elastic skin, built from within.

Ulthera's focused ultrasound triggers the skin's own collagen-remodelling response — a cumulative restoration of firmness that unfolds over weeks, not minutes.

— Phase 01

Thermal Stimulus

Focused ultrasound creates precise micro-coagulation points at a targeted depth, activating the skin's natural repair response.

— Phase 02

Neocollagenesis

New collagen and elastin begin to form around the treated points, gradually restoring structural support to the dermis.

— Phase 03

Tissue Remodelling

Over the following months, the new tissue continues to remodel — producing the firm, natural contour Ulthera is known for.

Frequently Asked

Ulthera® Q&A.

Questions we hear most often from patients considering Ulthera. For anything not covered here, please reach out via KakaoTalk or phone.

Q 01
Is there a risk of hollow cheeks or excessive fat loss?
+

The key is accurate assessment of each individual's facial structure before treatment. When Ulthera is applied without a precise understanding of facial type, there can be unintended consequences — including permanent volume loss in areas where fullness is needed.

Because fat distribution, dynamic facial lines (in expression), and static lines (at rest) differ from person to person, both states must be evaluated so that an accurate treatment design is completed before a single shot is delivered.

Q 02
How painful is Ulthera?
+

Ulthera delivers strong, point-like energy deep into the skin. Because individual pain thresholds vary, we offer a range of anaesthesia options — topical cream, nerve block, or sedation — selected during your consultation.

After treatment, a dull sensation may remain in parts of the face for a short period. This fades gradually over the following days.

Q 03
How many shots should I receive?
+

A balanced full-face result typically requires 400 shots or more, but the exact count depends on your individual facial type and the specific concerns you wish to address.

In many cases, combining Ulthera with complementary lifting treatments allows us to minimise total shots while concentrating precise energy where it is genuinely needed — a more refined approach than volume alone.
